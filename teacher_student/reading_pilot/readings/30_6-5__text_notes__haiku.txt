CONVENTIONS USED
- Rock classes: sst/sd/sand/sd-sst -> Sandstone; clyst/clay/sh/shale -> Shale; siltst/sltst -> Shale with Sandstone/Shale written in second_class (when siltstone is the second rock, or the main rock with no other second rock of a fifth or more); marl -> Marl; lst/ls/dol -> Limestone; coal -> Coal; tuff -> Tuff; very argillaceous sandstone -> Sandstone/Shale; conglomeratic sandstone -> Sandstone.
- Shares are summed by class before choosing the main rock. Ties go to the first-named rock. Second_class only if a second class has about a fifth (20 percent) or more; 15 percent or less is left empty.
- 'a/a' (as above): the composition is the one of the sample named in note; confidence medium.
- Traces (Tr) add no row and no second class.
- 'Shows' remarks (fluorescence, cut, gas, mud weight, W.T., wiper trip, drilling break, flows) are not rock descriptions and are not used.
