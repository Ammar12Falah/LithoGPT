How the rows were made
----------------------
Cuttings samples
 - One row per listed sample depth. A sample with one depth stands for the interval from the
   previous listed sample depth to its own depth. First sample (260 m): one 10 m step above.
 - "a/a" samples are copied as printed ("2044: a/a"); the note names the sample they repeat.
 - Class from the printed percentages: the rock with the largest share is the main rock;
   several claystones (grey / red-brown) are added together. Where two rocks have equal shares,
   or one rock has 50 % against two others of the Shale class, the first-named rock or the rock
   with the largest single share was taken and confidence set to medium.
 - Siltstone was put in class Shale. second_class = Sandstone/Shale was written where siltstone
   is the largest share of the sample, and also where claystone is the main rock and siltstone
   is 20 % or more and no other rock class reaches 20 %. Where sandstone is the main rock and
   siltstone / claystone / shale together are 20 % or more, second_class = Shale.
 - Samples said to be lost or missing have no row: 1927 ("sample lost"), 2014 ("lost sample"),
   2053 ("sample lost"), 3358 and 3361 ("missing due to pooh without circulation"). The sample
   after a lost one starts at the lost depth, so 1924-1927, 2011-2014, 2050-2053 and 3355-3361
   have no cuttings description (the formation summaries cover them).
 - Depths that are simply not in the list (no remark): 310, 1680 (1677 is printed instead),
   2506, 3280, 3490. The next sample was given the interval from the previous listed depth.
 - Cored interval: the list stops at 2872 ("CORED FROM 2871.5 - 2949.8 M") and starts again at
   2950; that sample was given the interval 2949.8-2950. Samples 2845-2854 are cuttings from
   the interval of cores 1 and 2 and were kept as rows.
Sidewall cores
 - One row per core, depth in both depth cells. Lists are in descending depth; "a/a" refers to
   the entry above it in the list (the deeper core). Sheets print only "Depth" with
   "K.B.E. 25 meters" in the header; depth_unit_ref written as "m KBE".
Cores
 - The core sheets are drawings (lithology column against a depth scale) with short descriptions
   beside the column. Only the core top/base and whole-metre ticks are printed as numbers. The
   tops and bases of the described units were read from the depth scale of the drawing and are
   approximate (about +/- 0.1 to 0.2 m). All core rows are therefore confidence medium (the thin
   coal bed of core 1 low). depth_unit_ref "m KB" is taken from the coring table on page 9
   ("Interval (m KB)"); the core sheets print only "DEPTH".
Formation summaries
 - The cuttings and cores cover almost the whole range, so the summaries are needed only for the
   small gaps named above. They were still all written (kind interval_summary, with their printed
   intervals) so that every geological description against depth is on record; they overlap the
   finer rows. Nordland Group (181-879) starts above the range and Cormorant Formation
   (3472-3550) ends below it; printed depths were kept. Group headings without a rock sentence
   of their own (Rogaland, Humber, Brent, Dunlin groups, Statfjord Formation) have no row.
 - Confidence: low where the sentence names several rocks as parts of the interval without
   shares; medium where a share word ("mostly") is used or where one rock is clearly the body
   and the others are given only as stringers / interbeds (Heather, Burton/Amundsen, Nansen).
   A stricter reading of the rule would put these three rows at low as well.
 - Cook Formation and Cormorant Formation headings print their depths without "KB". The Cook
   row has depth_unit_ref "m" (as printed). The Cormorant row carries "m KB" although its
   heading prints "(3472 m - 3550 m (T.D.))"; T.D. is given as 3550 m KB on page 7.
