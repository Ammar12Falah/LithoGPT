HOW THE ROWS WERE MADE
Cuttings samples
- A sample listed with one depth is given the interval from the previous listed sample depth to
  its own depth. First sample (430): top one 10 m step above (420). First sample after the cored
  interval (1611): top at 1610, the end of the cored interval named on the sheet. Where a grid
  depth is not listed (2154, 2268) the next sample is given the longer interval and this is said
  in the note. Sample 1845 starts at 1842 (the entry for 1842 is "Mostly cmt. Some med-crs sd
  grns, mica and coal" - no rock description, no row).
- Main rock = largest percentage. Two entries of the same rock (e.g. purple and green claystone,
  Cly (1) and Cly (2), two sandstones) are added. Equal shares: first-listed rock.
- second_class = a different rock of 20 percent or more.
- Siltstone: Shale with Sandstone/Shale in second_class (word map). Where siltstone is the
  second rock beside claystone or shale (20 percent or more) second_class is also
  Sandstone/Shale; where siltstone is the second rock beside sandstone, second_class is Shale.
  Where a different class (sandstone, limestone, marl) also reaches 20 percent, that class is
  put in second_class instead.
- Three-rock samples of the lower part (e.g. 40 Sst, 30 Sltst, 30 Clyst): the rock with the
  largest single share is taken (Sandstone), confidence medium; the note says that siltstone
  and claystone together are larger.
- "Lst/Marl" is read as Limestone and "Marl/Lst" as Marl (first-named rock). Lst/Marl called
  "chky" (1317 - 1326) is kept as Limestone, not Chalk.
- Tuff: samples 993 - 1017 (siltstone "tuff matr", claystone "tuffaceous") and 1047 - 1062
  (siltstone "tuffaceous") and the sidewall core at 924 ("Sltst ... tuffaceous") are classed
  Tuff because the word map says so for siltstone or claystone called tuffaceous without a
  weakening word. The rock itself is siltstone / claystone, which is put in second_class
  (Shale). These rows are marked medium. Samples 960 - 990 only say "(tuff?)" with a question
  mark and are kept as siltstone. The formation summary (p16) says "tuffaceous in parts".
- Bare "a/a" entries take the class of the entry they refer to; confidence medium.
- depth_unit_ref: the sample and sidewall core sheets head the depth column "DEPT" without a
  unit and give "K.B.E.: 23"; the geology chapter of the same report gives depths as "m RKB".
  Written as "m RKB (DEPT; K.B.E. 23)".
